Our Partners

The WAVE Aconcagua Partnership brings together key stakeholders working across three strategic levels—each playing a distinct role in driving the transition to more climate- and water-resilient production in the Aconcagua Valley.

  • Farm level – Fourteen producers represented by the Aconcagua Network.
  • Supply chain level – Retailers, importers, and exporters from Europe and the UK.
  • Enabling environment level – Partners for Water, the Dutch Government, and the Embassy of the Netherlands in Chile.
